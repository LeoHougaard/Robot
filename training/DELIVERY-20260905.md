## Experiment contract

V20 is a separate 426-input family with history indices `[0,10,20,21,22,23]`, actor and critic each `[128,128,128]`, a new optimizer/critic and a 50 Hz sampled observation contract. Its actor starts from the exact deployed V2 ONNX function, copied into the new layout with new columns zeroed. Independent ONNX-versus-PyTorch comparison passed 100 cases with maximum action difference 2.44e-6. This is actor initialization, not recovery of the original PPO checkpoint.

Training uses forward commands up to 0.10 m/s, lateral up to 0.08 m/s, yaw up to 0.30 rad/s, small posture commands and a fixed mild terrain distribution. It keeps body tracking dominant and the diagonal prior subordinate to actual progress. It has no self-righting resets. The fixed screen in `delivery_contract.py` tests forward/reverse 0.08 m/s, lateral 0.06 m/s, turns 0.20 rad/s, combined commands, stopping, posture and 20% current dropout. The initialized actor and trained candidates must face the same screen. Passing requires signed progress, proportional slow-command tracking, four-foot contact evidence, stability, finite complete metrics and no resets. Numeric results and actual video both control promotion. Wider commands are not certified by this initial screen.

The untrained transferred actor cycles some legs at a 0.18 m/s diagnostic request but achieves only about 0.006 m/s forward and moves the wrong way in reverse. That diagnostic establishes why copying the old actor alone is insufficient; it is not a passing baseline.

## Remaining uncertainty

The capture identifies effective encoder response under its recorded load, not continuous torque limits, contact geometry, ground truth body velocity or voltage dependence. The knee/hip coordinate relationship is retained; motor housing attachment remains a mechanical question before changing generalized torque coupling. Convex contact fidelity must be compared with the preserved SDF model. Real-world walking is unverified until the supervised physical test.

Research and historical intent: see [the review](SIM-TO-REAL-REVIEW-20260904.md). The approach follows measured actuator and delay modeling in [Tan et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.10332) and [Hwangbo et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.08652), with bounded parallel PPO and curriculum principles from [Rudin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.11978). These papers motivate the method; they do not establish transfer for this robot.
